### Known `_check07.py` false positives (no content defect)

- **P13** — the checker reads only the first `R` (6 kΩ → α = 240); the switched-in
  60 kΩ makes R_eq = 66 kΩ → α = 2640, which the solution states.
- **P28 / P29** — the dependent source −4i_1 cancels the 4 Ω series resistance, so the
  true α = 0 (undamped); the checker's naive R = 2 Ω gives α = 0.05. The solutions state
  R_eff = 0 explicitly.
- **P14, P15, P25, P27, P30** — design problems with symbolic R or L/C in the spec; the
  checker reports "find R/L/C → manual check". All five were hand-verified above
  (P14/P15 roots and constants, P25 full re-solve, P27 C > 0.8 F and i_L(t), P30
  inequality α/ω_d > ln 2 / 2π satisfied by the chosen R = 2.5 Ω).
