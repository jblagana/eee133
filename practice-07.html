P01 · Circuit Problem
📖 Adapted from Nils & Riedel 12e · original (method: NR Sec 8.4 four-step; no direct book figure)

At $t = 0$ a $12$ V dc source is switched in, in series with a $4$ $\Omega$ resistor $R_1$, to node A. From node A, a $2$ H inductor goes to the bottom rail, and a $6$ $\Omega$ resistor $R_2$ goes to node B; from node B, a $0.5$ F capacitor goes to the bottom rail. All elements are initially unenergized.

(A)Find $v_C(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.
KCL at both nodes reduces the circuit to $v_C'' + 1.4\,v_C' + 0.4\,v_C = 0$, so $\alpha = 0.7$, $\omega_0 = \sqrt{0.4} \approx 0.632$ rad/s: overdamped with roots $s_1 = -0.4$, $s_2 = -1$. Since the inductor is a short at dc, $v_C(\infty) = 0$, so $v_C(t) = Ae^{-0.4t} + Be^{-t}$. With $v_C(0^+) = 0$ and $v_C'(0^+) = v_A(0^+)/R_2C = 7.2/3 = 2.4$ V/s: $A + B = 0$ and $-0.4A - B = 2.4$ give $A = 4$, $B = -4$.
(B)Find $i_L(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.
From KCL at node A, $i_L = (12 - v_C)/4 - 1.25\,v_C'$. Substituting $v_C(t) = 4(e^{-0.4t} - e^{-t})$ and $v_C' = 4(-0.4e^{-0.4t} + e^{-t})$ gives $i_L(t) = 3 + e^{-0.4t} - 4e^{-t}$ A. Check: $i_L(0^+) = 0$ and $i_L(\infty) = 12/4 = 3$ A, as required (the inductor is a short at dc).
(C)Find the maximum value of $v_C(t)$ and the time at which it occurs.
Set $v_C'(t) = 4(-0.4e^{-0.4t} + e^{-t}) = 0$: $4e^{-t} = 1.6e^{-0.4t} \Rightarrow e^{0.6t} = 2.5 \Rightarrow t_p = \ln 2.5/0.6 \approx 1.53$ s. Then $v_C(t_p) = 4(e^{-0.612} - e^{-1.53}) \approx 4(0.542 - 0.216) \approx 1.30$ V.
P02 · Circuit Problem
📖 Adapted from Nils & Riedel 12e · Prob 8.9 (Sec 8.1)

A source-free parallel RLC circuit has $L = 10$ mH, $C = 1$ $\mu$F, and a resistor $R$ adjusted so that the characteristic roots are $-8000 \pm j6000$ rad/s. The initial conditions are $v(0) = 10$ V and $i_L(0) = 40$ mA.

(A)Find $R$.
The roots $-8000 \pm j6000$ give $\alpha = 8000$ Np/s. For a parallel RLC circuit $\alpha = 1/(2RC)$, so $R = 1/(2C\alpha) = 1/(2 \times 10^{-6} \times 8000) = 62.5$ $\Omega$. (Consistency: $\omega_0 = \sqrt{\alpha^2 + \omega_d^2} = 10^4$ rad/s $= 1/\sqrt{LC}$.)
(B)Find $\dfrac{dv}{dt}\big|_{0^+}$.
KCL at $0^+$ (downward branch currents): $i_C(0^+) = -v(0)/R - i_L(0^+) = -10/62.5 - 0.04 = -0.20$ A, so $dv/dt|_{0^+} = i_C(0^+)/C = -0.20/10^{-6} = -2.0 \times 10^5$ V/s.
(C)Find the constants $B_1$ and $B_2$ in $v(t) = e^{-\alpha t}(B_1\cos\omega_d t + B_2\sin\omega_d t)$.
$B_1 = v(0) = 10$. Differentiating, $v'(0^+) = -\alpha B_1 + \omega_d B_2$, so $-2.0 \times 10^5 = -8000 \times 10 + 6000\,B_2 \Rightarrow B_2 = (-2.0 \times 10^5 + 8 \times 10^4)/6000 = -20$. Hence $v(t) = e^{-8000t}(10\cos 6000t - 20\sin 6000t)$ V.
(D)Find $i_L(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.
$i_L(t) = -v/R - C\,dv/dt$. With $v = e^{-8000t}(10\cos 6000t - 20\sin 6000t)$: $-v/R = e^{-8000t}(-0.16\cos 6000t + 0.32\sin 6000t)$ A and $-C\,v' = e^{-8000t}(0.20\cos 6000t - 0.10\sin 6000t)$ A; summing, $i_L(t) = e^{-8000t}(0.04\cos 6000t + 0.22\sin 6000t)$ A. Check: $i_L(0^+) = 0.04$ A $= i_L(0)$.
P03 · Circuit Problem
📖 Adapted from Nils & Riedel 12e · Prob 8.10 (Sec 8.1)

A source-free parallel RLC circuit has $L = 0.4$ H and $C = 10$ $\mu$F, and its resistance is adjusted for critical damping. The initial energy stored in the circuit is 25 mJ, distributed equally between the inductor and the capacitor, with $v(0) = 50$ V and $i_L(0) = 0.25$ A.

(A)Find $R$.
$\omega_0 = 1/\sqrt{LC} = 1/\sqrt{0.4 \times 10^{-5}} = 500$ rad/s. Critical damping requires $\alpha = \omega_0 = 500$ Np/s, so $R = 1/(2C\alpha) = 1/(2 \times 10^{-5} \times 500) = 100$ $\Omega$.
(B)Find $v(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.
With $v(t) = (A_1 + A_2 t)e^{-500t}$: $A_1 = v(0) = 50$. KCL at $0^+$ gives $i_C(0^+) = -50/100 - 0.25 = -0.75$ A, so $v'(0^+) = -75000$ V/s. Then $v'(0^+) = A_2 - 500A_1 \Rightarrow A_2 = -75000 + 25000 = -50000$, giving $v(t) = (50 - 50000\,t)e^{-500t}$ V.
(C)Find $i_L(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.
$i_L(t) = i_L(0) + \tfrac{1}{L}\int_0^t v\,d\tau = (0.25 + 250\,t)e^{-500t}$ A (equivalently $i_L = -v/R - C\,v'$). Check: $i_L(0^+) = 0.25$ A and $i_L(\infty) = 0$.
(D)Find $i_R(0^+)$.
The resistor is in parallel with the capacitor, so $i_R(0^+) = v(0)/R = 50/100 = 0.5$ A.
P04 · Circuit Problem
📖 Adapted from Nils & Riedel 12e · Prob 8.5 (Sec 8.1)

A source-free parallel RLC circuit has $R = 2$ k$\Omega$, $L = 250$ mH, and $C = 10$ nF. The initial conditions are $i_L(0) = -30$ mA and $v_C(0) = 90$ V.

(A)Find $i_R(0^+)$ and $i_C(0^+)$.
$i_R(0^+) = v_C(0)/R = 90/2000 = 45$ mA. KCL with downward branch currents: $i_C(0^+) = -i_R(0^+) - i_L(0^+) = -45 - (-30) = -15$ mA.
(B)Find $v_C(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.
$\alpha = 1/(2RC) = 25000$ Np/s, $\omega_0 = 1/\sqrt{LC} = 20000$ rad/s: overdamped, roots $s_1 = -10000$, $s_2 = -40000$. So $v_C = Ae^{-10000t} + Be^{-40000t}$ with $A + B = 90$ and $v_C'(0^+) = i_C(0^+)/C = -1.5 \times 10^6$ V/s: $-10000A - 40000B = -1.5 \times 10^6 \Rightarrow A + 4B = 150$. Hence $B = 20$, $A = 70$.
(C)Find $i_L(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.
$i_L(t) = -v_C/R - C\,dv_C/dt$. Differentiating $v_C$ and substituting gives $i_L(t) = -(0.028\,e^{-10000t} + 0.002\,e^{-40000t})$ A. Check: $i_L(0^+) = -(0.028 + 0.002) = -30$ mA and $i_L(\infty) = 0$.
P05 · Circuit Problem
📖 Adapted from Nils & Riedel 12e · Prob 8.6 (Sec 8.1)

A source-free parallel RLC circuit has $L = 50$ mH and $C = 50$ nF, and its resistance is chosen so that the response is critically damped. The initial conditions are $v(0) = 60$ V and $i_L(0) = 0$.

(A)Find the resistance $R$.
$\omega_0 = 1/\sqrt{LC} = 1/\sqrt{0.05 \times 5 \times 10^{-8}} = 20000$ rad/s. Critical damping: $\alpha = \omega_0 = 20000$ Np/s, so $R = 1/(2C\alpha) = 1/(2 \times 5 \times 10^{-8} \times 20000) = 500$ $\Omega$.
(B)Find $v(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.
$v(t) = (A_1 + A_2t)e^{-20000t}$ with $A_1 = 60$. KCL at $0^+$: $i_C(0^+) = -60/500 - 0 = -0.12$ A, so $v'(0^+) = -2.4 \times 10^6$ V/s and $A_2 = v'(0^+) + 20000A_1 = -2.4 \times 10^6 + 1.2 \times 10^6 = -1.2 \times 10^6$. Hence $v(t) = 60(1 - 20000\,t)e^{-20000t}$ V.
(C)Find $i_L(t)$ for $t \ge 0$ and its peak value.
$i_L(t) = -v/R - C\,dv/dt = 1200\,t\,e^{-20000t}$ A. The peak occurs where $di_L/dt = 1200e^{-20000t}(1 - 20000t) = 0$, i.e. $t = 1/20000 = 50$ $\mu$s, with $i_{L,\max} = 1200 \times 50 \times 10^{-6}/e = 0.06/e \approx 22.1$ mA.

P06 · Circuit Problem
📖 Adapted from Hayt 8e · Sec 9.6, worked circuit of Examples 9.10-9.11 (topology reconstructed from the worked text)

In the two-loop RLC circuit shown, a current source $4u(t)$ A feeds node A and a $5$ A current source feeds node B; $R = 30$ $\Omega$ connects A to B, $L = 3$ H connects A to the bottom rail, and $C = 1/27$ F connects B to the bottom rail. For $t < 0$ the circuit is in dc steady state.

(A)Find $v_C(0^+)$ and $i_L(0^+)$.
For $t < 0$ the $4u(t)$ source is off. In dc steady state the inductor is a short (the 5 A source flows through it: $i_L(0^-) = 5$ A) and the capacitor is an open. Then $i_R(0^-) = -5$ A, $v_R(0^-) = -150$ V, and $v_C(0^-) = v_A(0^-) - v_R(0^-) = 0 - (-150) = 150$ V. By continuity: $v_C(0^+) = 150$ V, $i_L(0^+) = 5$ A.
(B)Find $v_C(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.
With the sources off, the loop is a series RLC: $\alpha = R/(2L) = 5$ Np/s, $\omega_0 = 1/\sqrt{LC} = 3$ rad/s, roots $s_1 = -1$, $s_2 = -9$. Final values: $i_L(\infty) = 9$ A, $v_C(\infty) = 150$ V. So $v_C = 150 + Ae^{-t} + Be^{-9t}$ with $A + B = v_C(0^+) - 150 = 0$ and $v_C'(0^+) = i_C(0^+)/C = 4/(1/27) = 108$ V/s: $-A - 9B = 108 \Rightarrow A = 13.5$, $B = -13.5$.
(C)Find $i_L(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.
$i_C(t) = C\,v_C'(t) = -0.5\,e^{-t} + 4.5\,e^{-9t}$ A. From KCL, $i_R = i_C - 5$ and $i_L = 4 - i_R = 9 - i_C = 9 + 0.5\,e^{-t} - 4.5\,e^{-9t}$ A. Check: $i_L(0^+) = 5$ A and $di_L/dt|_{0^+} = v_L(0^+)/L = 120/3 = 40$ A/s.
P07 · Circuit Problem
📖 Adapted from Hayt 8e · Example 9.10 (Sec 9.6; same circuit as [H15])

In the same two-source RLC circuit as the previous problem ($R = 30$ $\Omega$ between nodes A and B, $L = 3$ H from A to the rail, $C = 1/27$ F from B to the rail, sources $4u(t)$ A into A and $5$ A into B, dc steady state for $t < 0$), determine at $t = 0^+$ the first derivatives of all six branch variables: $\dfrac{di_L}{dt}$, $\dfrac{dv_C}{dt}$, $\dfrac{di_R}{dt}$, $\dfrac{dv_R}{dt}$, $\dfrac{di_C}{dt}$, and $\dfrac{dv_L}{dt}$.

(A)Find $di_L/dt\big|_{0^+}$ and $dv_C/dt\big|_{0^+}$.
From the $0^+$ values: $v_L(0^+) = v_C(0^+) + v_R(0^+) = 150 + 30(-1) = 120$ V and $i_C(0^+) = i_R(0^+) + 5 = -1 + 5 = 4$ A. Hence $di_L/dt|_{0^+} = v_L(0^+)/L = 120/3 = 40$ A/s and $dv_C/dt|_{0^+} = i_C(0^+)/C = 4/(1/27) = 108$ V/s.
(B)Find $di_R/dt\big|_{0^+}$ and $dv_R/dt\big|_{0^+}$.
The derivatives obey KCL too: at node A, $0 = di_L/dt + di_R/dt$ (the source is constant), so $di_R/dt|_{0^+} = -40$ A/s. Then $dv_R/dt|_{0^+} = R\,di_R/dt = 30 \times (-40) = -1200$ V/s.
(C)Find $di_C/dt\big|_{0^+}$.
At node B the 5 A source is constant, so $di_C/dt|_{0^+} = di_R/dt|_{0^+} = -40$ A/s.
(D)Find $dv_L/dt\big|_{0^+}$.
Around the loop $v_L = v_C + v_R$ for all $t$, so $dv_L/dt|_{0^+} = dv_C/dt + dv_R/dt = 108 - 1200 = -1092$ V/s.
P08 · Circuit Problem
📖 Adapted from Nils & Riedel 12e · Prob 8.7 (Sec 8.1)

A source-free parallel RLC circuit has $R = 12500/3$ $\Omega$, $L = 250$ mH, and $C = 10$ nF, with initial conditions $v(0) = 90$ V and $i_L(0) = -34$ mA. Find $v(t)$ and $i_L(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.

(A)Find $v(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.
$\alpha = 1/(2RC) = 12000$ Np/s, $\omega_0 = 1/\sqrt{LC} = 20000$ rad/s: underdamped, $\omega_d = \sqrt{20000^2 - 12000^2} = 16000$ rad/s. KCL at $0^+$: $i_C(0^+) = -90/(12500/3) + 0.034 = 0.0124$ A, so $v'(0^+) = i_C(0^+)/C = 1.24 \times 10^6$ V/s. With $B_1 = v(0) = 90$ and $v'(0^+) = -12000B_1 + 16000B_2$: $B_2 = (1.24 \times 10^6 + 1.08 \times 10^6)/16000 = 145$.
(B)Find $i_L(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.
$i_L(t) = -v/R - C\,dv/dt$. Substituting $v(t) = e^{-12000t}(90\cos 16000t + 145\sin 16000t)$ gives $i_L(t) = -e^{-12000t}(0.034\cos 16000t + 0.003\sin 16000t)$ A. Check: $i_L(0^+) = -0.034$ A $= i_L(0)$ and $i_L(\infty) = 0$.
P09 · Circuit Problem
📖 Adapted from Nils & Riedel 12e · Prob 8.49 (Sec 8.3)

The switch has been in position a for a long time, where a $100$ V source charged the $2$ mF capacitor through a $15$ k$\Omega$ resistor (the inductor carried no current), and moves to position b at $t = 0$. For $t \ge 0$ the source-free loop consists of $R = 3.5$ $\Omega$, $L = 5$ mH, and $C = 2$ mF.

(A)Find $v_C(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.
$v_C(0^+) = 100$ V, $i_L(0^+) = 0$. The loop is source-free series RLC: $\alpha = R/(2L) = 350$ Np/s, $\omega_0 = 1/\sqrt{LC} \approx 316.2$ rad/s: overdamped, roots $s_1 = -200$, $s_2 = -500$. So $v_C = Ae^{-200t} + Be^{-500t}$ with $A + B = 100$ and $v_C'(0^+) = i_C(0^+)/C = 0$: $-200A - 500B = 0 \Rightarrow A = -2.5B$. Hence $B = -200/3$, $A = 500/3$ (approx. $166.7\,e^{-200t} - 66.7\,e^{-500t}$ V).
(B)Find $i_L(t)$ for $t \ge 0$, with $i_L$ in the direction of capacitor discharge.
$i_L(t) = C\,dv_C/dt = 2 \times 10^{-3}\,\tfrac{d}{dt}\left(\tfrac{500}{3}e^{-200t} - \tfrac{200}{3}e^{-500t}\right) = \tfrac{200}{3}\left(e^{-500t} - e^{-200t}\right)$ A (approx. $66.7(e^{-500t} - e^{-200t})$ A). Check: $i_L(0) = 0$ and $i_L(\infty) = 0$.

📖 Adapted from Nils & Riedel 12e · Prob 8.17 (Sec 8.3)

P14 · Circuit Problem
📖 Adapted from Nils & Riedel 12e · Prob 8.41 (Sec 8.4)

A source-free series RLC circuit has a capacitor C = 31.25 nF. The initial energy stored in the capacitor is 9 µJ, and the initial inductor current is zero. The characteristic roots of the circuit are -4000 and -16000 rad/s.

(A)Find $R$ and $L$.
$\alpha = (4000 + 16000)/2 = 10000$ Np/s and $\omega_0 = \sqrt{4000 \times 16000} = 8000$ rad/s. Then $L = 1/(\omega_0^2 C) = 1/(64 \times 10^6 \times 31.25 \times 10^{-9}) = 0.5$ H and $R = 2L\alpha = 2(0.5)(10000) = 10$ k$\Omega$.
(B)Find $i(0^+)$ and $di/dt\big|_{0^+}$.
$i(0^+) = i_L(0) = 0$. From $W_C = 9$ $\mu$J, $v_C(0) = \sqrt{2W_C/C} = \sqrt{18 \times 10^{-6}/31.25 \times 10^{-9}} = 24$ V. KVL at $0^+$: $v_L(0^+) = -v_C(0^+) - i(0)R = -24$ V, so $di/dt|_{0^+} = v_L(0^+)/L = -24/0.5 = -48$ A/s.
(C)Find $i(t)$ for $t \ge 0$.
Overdamped: $i(t) = A_1e^{-4000t} + A_2e^{-16000t}$. $i(0) = 0 \Rightarrow A_2 = -A_1$, and $di/dt|_{0^+} = -4000A_1 - 16000A_2 = 12000A_1 = -48$ gives $A_1 = -4$ mA, $A_2 = +4$ mA: $i(t) = 4(e^{-16000t} - e^{-4000t})$ mA.
(D)Find the time at which the current peaks.
$di/dt = 0 \Rightarrow -16000 \times 4\,e^{-16000t} + 4000 \times 4\,e^{-4000t} = 0 \Rightarrow e^{-4000t} = 4e^{-16000t} \Rightarrow 12000t = \ln 4$, so $t_{\text{peak}} = \ln 4/12000 \approx 115.5$ $\mu$s.
(E)Find the peak current value.

P16 · Circuit Problem
📖 Adapted from Nils & Riedel 12e · Prob 8.38 (Sec 8.4)

A series RLC circuit consists of a 250 V DC source, a resistor R = 1 kΩ, an inductor L = 1.6 H, and a capacitor C = 10 mF. The circuit has been in steady state with the switch open (capacitor uncharged, inductor current zero). At t = 0, the switch closes.

(A)Find the net energy delivered to the inductor.
$\Delta W_L = \tfrac{1}{2}L[i(\infty)^2 - i(0)^2]$: both $i(0^-) = 0$ and $i(\infty) = 0$ (at dc the capacitor is an open circuit), so the net inductor energy change is 0 J.
(B)Find the energy dissipated by the resistor.
The source delivers $W_S = 250\,[Cv_C(\infty) - 0] = 250 \times 2.5 = 625$ J and the capacitor stores $W_C = \tfrac{1}{2}(0.01)(250)^2 = 312.5$ J, so by conservation $W_R = W_S - W_C - \Delta W_L = 625 - 312.5 - 0 = 312.5$ J (independent of the damping type).
(C)Find the net energy delivered to the capacitor.
$\Delta W_C = \tfrac{1}{2}C[v_C(\infty)^2 - v_C(0)^2]$: at dc steady state the inductor is a short, so the full 250 V appears across the capacitor and $\Delta W_C = \tfrac{1}{2}(0.01)(250)^2 - 0 = 312.5$ J.
(D)Find the total energy delivered by the source.
$W_S = \int_0^\infty v_s i\,dt = 250\,[q(\infty) - q(0)] = 250\,[Cv_C(\infty) - 0] = 250 \times (0.01 \times 250) = 625$ J.
(E)Check energy conservation.
Source delivery = resistor dissipation + net stored energy: $625 = 312.5 + (0 + 312.5)$. Energy is conserved exactly.
