P01 · Circuit Problem

A 100 µF capacitor is connected in series with a 5 kΩ resistor to a 12 V DC source. The switch closes at t=0. If the capacitor is initially uncharged, find the time constant τ and the capacitor voltage v_C(t) for t ≥ 0.

1. Initial condition: $v_C(0^+) = v_C(0^-) = 0$ V.
2. Final condition: At steady state, C is open, so $v_C(\infty) = 12$ V.
3. Time constant: $R_{th} = 5$ kΩ. $\tau = R_{th}C = (5 \times 10^3)(100 \times 10^{-6}) = 0.5$ s.
4. Response: $v_C(t) = 12 + (0 - 12)e^{-t/0.5} = 12(1 - e^{-2t})$ V.
Answer: τ = 0.5 s, v_C(t) = 12(1 - e^{-2t}) V
P02 · Circuit Problem

A 2 H inductor is connected in series with a 4 Ω resistor to a 20 V DC source. The switch closes at t=0. If the inductor current is initially zero, find the time constant τ and the inductor current i_L(t) for t ≥ 0.

1. Initial condition: $i_L(0^+) = i_L(0^-) = 0$ A.
2. Final condition: At steady state, L is short, so $i_L(\infty) = 20/4 = 5$ A.
3. Time constant: $R_{th} = 4$ Ω. $\tau = L/R_{th} = 2/4 = 0.5$ s.
4. Response: $i_L(t) = 5 + (0 - 5)e^{-t/0.5} = 5(1 - e^{-2t})$ A.
Answer: τ = 0.5 s, i_L(t) = 5(1 - e^{-2t}) A

P05 · Circuit Problem

A circuit consists of a 12 V source in series with a 4 kΩ resistor, connected to a node. From this node, a 2 kΩ resistor goes to ground, and a 0.5 µF capacitor goes to ground. The switch closes at t=0. Find the time constant τ and the capacitor voltage v_C(t) for t ≥ 0, assuming the capacitor is initially uncharged.

1. Initial condition: $v_C(0^+) = 0$ V.
2. Final condition: At steady state, C is open. The circuit is a voltage divider: $v_C(\infty) = 12 \times \frac{2}{4+2} = 4$ V.
3. Time constant: Deactivate source (short). $R_{th}$ seen by C is $4$ kΩ in parallel with $2$ kΩ: $R_{th} = \frac{4 \times 2}{4 + 2} = 1.333$ kΩ. $\tau = (1.333 \times 10^3)(0.5 \times 10^{-6}) = 0.667$ ms.
4. Response: $v_C(t) = 4 + (0 - 4)e^{-t/0.667ms} = 4(1 - e^{-1500t})$ V.
Answer: τ = 0.667 ms, v_C(t) = 4(1 - e^{-1500t}) V
P06 · Circuit Problem

A 12 V source is in series with a 6 Ω resistor, connected to an inductor L=12 H to ground. A 2 A current source is also connected in parallel with the inductor, both switched on at t=0. Find the time constant τ and the inductor current i_L(t) for t ≥ 0.

1. Initial condition: $i_L(0^+) = 0$ A.
2. Final condition: L is short. KCL at node: $i_L(\infty) = \frac{12}{6} + 2 = 4$ A.
3. Time constant: Deactivate sources (V short, I open). $R_{th} = 6$ Ω. $\tau = L/R_{th} = 12/6 = 2$ s.
4. Response: $i_L(t) = 4 + (0 - 4)e^{-t/2} = 4(1 - e^{-0.5t})$ A.
Answer: τ = 2 s, i_L(t) = 4(1 - e^{-0.5t}) A
