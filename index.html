2 Formula quick-reference

The ten formulas that recur across all five lectures. Keep this page open while you study.

Capacitor V-I
\( i(t) = C\,\dfrac{dv(t)}{dt} \)

Current follows the rate of change of voltage.

Inductor V-I
\( v(t) = L\,\dfrac{di(t)}{dt} \)

Voltage follows the rate of change of current.

Stored energy
\( w_C = \tfrac{1}{2}CV^2 \),   \( w_L = \tfrac{1}{2}Li^2 \)

Always positive — energy is stored, never burned.

Combinations
\(C\): \(\tfrac{1}{C_{eq}}=\textstyle\sum \tfrac{1}{C_k}\) (ser.)
\(L\): \(L_{eq}=\textstyle\sum L_k\) (ser.)

Capacitors and inductors combine oppositely.

Time constant
\( \tau_{RC} = RC \),   \( \tau_{RL} = \dfrac{L}{R} \)

\(R\) = Thévenin resistance seen by the element. Practically done at \(5\tau\).

General response
\( x(t) = x(\infty) + \big[x(0^+) - x(\infty)\big]\,e^{-t/\tau} \)
switching at t = t₀\( x(t) = x(\infty) + \big[x(t_0^+) - x(\infty)\big]\,e^{-(t-t_0)/\tau} \)

The single most important formula of Lectures 3–4. If the switch fires at \(t_0\), restart the clock there: \(0^+ \to t_0^+\), \(t \to t - t_0\).

Unit step
\( u(t) = 0 \ (t<0),\; 1 \ (t \ge 0) \)

Models abrupt switching; pulses = shifted steps.

3-dB cutoff
\( \omega_c = \dfrac{1}{RC} \)
\(f_c = \dfrac{1}{2\pi RC}\)

Same formula for HP and LP — different sides of the band.

Damping factor
\( \alpha_{ser} = \tfrac{R}{2L} \),   \( \alpha_{par} = \dfrac{1}{2RC} \)

Series: more R damps more. Parallel: more R damps less.

Natural frequencies
\( \omega_0 = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{LC}} \)
\( s_{1,2} = -\alpha \pm \sqrt{\alpha^2 - \omega_0^2} \)

The roots set the regime: real, equal, or complex.
